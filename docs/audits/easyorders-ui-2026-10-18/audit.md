## Confirmed finding

The real Orders toolbar selector was too narrow for the current UI; it now resolves by exact accessible name for both observed toolbar variants. Local fixes also cover current real-order readiness/menu structure, the page-size combobox, and notification freshness/link handling. Focused suites: `tests/easy-orders-ui-audit-regression.test.js`, `tests/easy-orders-export-dialog.test.js`, and `tests/affiliate-recovery.test.js`.

## Limits

Apply and one final modal Export were exercised on both real and missed Orders for Oct 10–18; both downloads parsed as XLSX, but the missed filename reports Oct 9 as its start date and the data range was not inspected. Date inputs after filter selection remain uncaptured; two attempts to capture the filtered state were blocked by the computer-use capture tool. Page size was smoke-tested at 100 and restored to 25. Edit dialogs were opened and canceled. One real resend and one missed conversion were submitted under the user's Abdo-account authorization; no Delete, cart upload, or other-account action occurred. The current-day verification-end and manual duplicate-preflight fixes have focused automated coverage, but were not followed by another live recovery action. Dashboard Export Data produced a readable workbook earlier; the later post-conversion Dashboard fetch also confirmed one current-day matching row without exposing row values.
